# TEACHER_PROFILE_NOTE
IMPORTANT CONTEXT:
- This is a 25-minute online 1-on-1 speaking class.
- The teacher is a Filipino ESL teacher, typically B1-B2 level English.
- Instructions must be simple and clear — the teacher should understand at a glance what to do.
- Avoid complex metalanguage or long teacher scripts.
- Keep each slide to 3-4 interaction points maximum.
- No large blocks of text to read aloud.
- The ENTIRE lesson has a MAXIMUM of 6 sections. Every section must include at least one student speaking turn.
- DO NOT add grammar_focus, speaking_chain, or quick_check sections under any circumstances.
- CRITICAL — 1-on-1 ONLY: There are exactly 2 people in class: the Student and the Tutor. NEVER write instructions involving groups, pairs, classmates, or partners. All activities must work as Student vs. Tutor. Replace any "with a partner" or "group" language with "with your tutor".
