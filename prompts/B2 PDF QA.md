### 2. Slide 10: The "Rewrite Mission" Rule (Updated Format)

### 3. Slide 7: The "Mode & Logic" Scan (PREP vs STAR)
*   **Focus:** Speaking Strategy.
*   **Check:** Identify the Lesson Mode.
    *   **IF Argument/Opinion:** Must use **PREP Model**.

    *   **IF Narrative/Story:** Must use **STAR Model** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
    *   ❌ **FAIL:** Using PREP for a story, or STAR for a debate.

### 4. Slide 2 & 9: The "Critical Depth" Rule (Restored)
*   **Focus:** Warm Up & Real-World Scenario.
*   **Check:** Do the prompts force **Critical Evaluation** (Pros/Cons, Conditions)?

### 5. Slide 5 & 9: The "Hedging & Flow" Rule (Restored)
*   **Focus:** Conversation Skills (Slide 5) & Scenario (Slide 9).
*   **Check (Slide 5):** Does the Drill enforce **Hedging/Stance Markers**? (e.g., "Admittedly," "From a strategic perspective").
*   **Check (Slide 9):** Are instructions given to use **Discourse Markers** (e.g., "To be honest," "Regarding this") to prevent robotic dialogue?

### 6. Slide 3 & 4: The "Label & Nuance" Protocol (UPDATED)
*   **Focus:** Language Input.
*   **Check 1 (Label Accuracy):** Does the example sentence use the label **"🗣️ Example:"**?

*   **Check 2 (Anti-Leak):** Is the Nuance Check a **Question**? (No answers allowed).
*   **Check 3 (Simplicity):** Is the *question itself* written in simple A2/B1 English?
    *   ❌ **FAIL:** "Does this word connote a formal register?" (Too complex).
    *   ✅ **PASS:** "Do you use this with your boss or your friend?" (Simple).

### 7. Slide 6: The "Rhetorical Effect" Rule
